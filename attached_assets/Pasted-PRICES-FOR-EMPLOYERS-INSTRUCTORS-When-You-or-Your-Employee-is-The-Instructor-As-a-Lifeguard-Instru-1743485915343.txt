PRICES FOR EMPLOYERS & INSTRUCTORS
When You or Your Employee is The Instructor
As a Lifeguard Instructor, Water Safety Swim Instructor Trainer, or CPR & First Aid Instructor, you may buy hugely discounted certification courses for the candidates you intend to certify. 

Each of your candidates need a Home-Study Course Ticket to access their required Home-Study Course. 
The more tickets you buy the lower your price per ticket.
Your tickets are freely transferable and reusable. You do not lose any money if your candidates quit, fail, or cancel because you can simply reassign the ticket to another candidate. The new candidate will start at the beginning of the Home-Study Course. You may repeat this process of transferring or reusing the ticket, until the ticket eventually generates a certification. 
Save between 65-85% per ticket. 
Have no worries, your tickets never expire!
