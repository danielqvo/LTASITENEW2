Bloodborne Pathogens Certification Course
This program on bloodborne pathogens (BBP) educates participants on health issues by identifying and managing infectious agents in human blood. This instruction is mandated yearly across certain industries by the U.S. Occupational and Safety Health Administration (OSHA). We demonstrate methods to recognize BBPs and take appropriate action to assist those in need without putting oneself at risk. Completing this program and obtaining certification affords global recognition of your competence in handling blood.

The program offers insights into bloodborne pathogens (BBPs) and delineates the goals of consistent BBP training. We introduce you to an ‘exposure control plan’ (ECP) that outlines healthcare workers’ approach to managing pathogen exposure. We categorize various BBPs by exploring prevalent examples such as hepatitis B and C, and HIV. The program details the four principal steps for reacting to pathogen exposure and encompasses universal safety measures, hygiene practices, and basic preventive actions.

Furthermore, we guide you through protective strategies that shield against BBP transmission. We examine suitable personal protective gear (PPE), the secure disposal of ‘sharps’ (such as needles and scalpels), and the essential practices, procedures, and policies a workplace must implement for the cleanup of blood and other bodily fluids. We delve into the Occupational and Safety Health Administration (OSHA) in the United States to clarify applicable entities and their regulatory requirements.

This program is tailored for healthcare professionals who seek to mitigate health risks whilst providing care to others. It instructs on minimizing disease spread during professional duties, thereby safeguarding oneself, peers, patients, and relatives from potential contagion. Acquiring this knowledge not only safeguards your well-being but also ensures uninterrupted professional practice and is a prerequisite for employment in laboratory or medical settings. Engaging in blood-related tasks is a vital component of contemporary healthcare; therefore, enrolling in this program enhances your skill set and adds a noteworthy qualification to your curriculum vitae.
